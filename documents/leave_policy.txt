HRMS Demo Company - Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave

Employees who are over 5 years of experience are entitled to 28 calendar days and employees below 5 years of experience are entitles to 21 calendar days of annual leave for each completed year of service.

Annual leave requests should normally be submitted at least 7 working days before the intended leave date.

All annual leave requests require approval from the employee's manager. In the absence of Manager the employee should get the approval from the reporting person of that employee.

2. Sick Leave

Employees may request sick leave when they are unable to work because of illness.
An Employee gets 14 days of paid sick leave in a year form the company after submitting the authorized sick leave letter from the physician

Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

Medical documentation may be required according to company policy to get the paid sick leave

3. Emergency Leave

Emergency leave may be requested when an unexpected personal or family emergency prevents an employee from attending work.

The minimum emergency leave requesting by the employee should be of 7 days.
Employees should notify their manager as soon as possible.

4. Leave Carry Forward

Unused annual leave may be carried forward to the following year, subject to the company's applicable leave rules.

5. Leave Requests

Employees must submit leave requests through the HRMS system.

The employee's manager reviews the request before it is approved or rejected.
